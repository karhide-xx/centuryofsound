    Ethnographic recordings from the Pitt Rivers Museum's collections - remixed and reimagined

    A Century of Sounds is a partnership between the Pitt Rivers Museum and Cities and Memory, one of the world's largest sound projects.

    100 sound recordings from all over the world have been reimagined by artists and musicians into brand new compositions, each giving their own creative and emotional response to the original sound - with an amazing array of different styles and approaches.

    To the left, you will find in each case the original recording first, with the reimagined composition based on it below.

    Tap screen to hear sounds

    How to use

    • Press round play buttons to start and pause sounds
    • Move through the sounds using the arrow buttons at the bottom
    • Use the Search tab to explore using keywords, artist names etc.
    • Use the Filter tab to explore sounds via date, country and other options
    • Tap the info button [i] to learn more about each sound

    Access the full project on your own device by scanning the QR code below.

    Visit citiesandmemory.com/century-sounds to continue listening at home.
